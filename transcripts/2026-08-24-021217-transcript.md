● Plan written. The design in brief:

  Six modules — identity (Organisation, User), skill (the catalogue), crew
  (CrewMember, CrewSkill), mission (Mission, MissionApproval, CrewRequirement,
  RequiredSkill), assignment (Assignment), and matching (no entities — it just

  Three things worth flagging before you approve:

  The accept-time overlap check. Your two answers interact: offers don't block,
  but accepted missions can't overlap. So two Mission Leads can both offer the
  same person for clashing dates and neither offer is invalid — the second
  acceptance has to fail. That also means it can't be a plain unique constraint;
  it needs a Postgres exclusion constraint or a transactional check.

  MissionApproval as its own entity. The lifecycle allows REJECTED → PLAN →
  resubmit, which can loop. A single rejectionReason field on Mission would be
